FAQ

Common questions.

Do you provide mobiles on EMI?

EMI/finance options can be shown for eligible customers and products. Final approval, tenure, charges and down payment depend on the financing partner and applicable scheme.

Is Bajaj Finance available on every phone?

Not necessarily. Availability can vary by store participation, product, scheme and customer eligibility. Confirm the current option with the shop before purchase.

Can I exchange my old phone?

You can advertise exchange assistance here. Final exchange value should be decided after checking model, age, condition, display, battery and device functionality.

Are online prices and shop prices always the same?

No. Smartphone prices and offers change frequently. The final invoice price should be confirmed with the shop on the day of purchase.

Can I reserve a phone on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on this website to ask the shop about current stock, color, storage and booking policy.
